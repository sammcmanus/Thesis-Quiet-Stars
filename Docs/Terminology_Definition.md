# Terminology Definitions

| **Term** | **Definition** |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Bench Player** | A player who begins the game as a substitute and enters later; often plays fewer minutes. |
| **BPM (Box Plus/Minus)** | The overall version of OBPM + DBPM. It estimates a player’s total contribution (offense + defense) per 100 possessions compared to an average player. |
| **DBPM (Defensive Box Plus/Minus)** | An estimate of a player’s defensive impact on team performance per 100 possessions, based on box score data. Positive values indicate better-than-average defenders; negative values indicate below-average defense. |
| **Experience** | The number of seasons a player has played in the NBA. Used to classify players as veterans or non-veterans. |
| **Losses (L)** | The number of games a team loses in a season. |
| **MP (Minutes Played)** | The total number of minutes a player spends on the court during a season or game. Often used as a weight when calculating averages to give more influence to players with more playing time. |
| **OBPM (Offensive Box Plus/Minus)** | Similar to DBPM, but measures offensive contribution. High OBPM means strong scoring or playmaking value. |
| **PER (Player Efficiency Rating)** | A single-number measure developed by John Hollinger that summarizes a player’s per-minute statistical production. It accounts for positive stats (points, assists, rebounds, etc.) and negative ones (turnovers, missed shots, fouls). The league average is always set to 15.0. |
| **Role** | Categorization of a player’s role on their team, typically coded as: S = Starter, R = Role Player, B = Bench. |
| **Role Player** | A non-star player who contributes in specific, consistent ways — such as defense, rebounding, or shooting — often supporting starters. |
| **Starter** | A player who begins the game on the court and usually plays the most minutes. |
| **TS% (True Shooting Percentage)** | A measure of overall shooting efficiency that includes field goals, three-pointers, and free throws. It represents how efficiently a player scores considering all shot types. Formula: TS% = Points / (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)). |
| **USG% (Usage Percentage)** | The percentage of team plays a player is involved in while on the floor, ending with a shot attempt, turnover, or drawn foul. High usage usually means the player is a key offensive option. |
| **Veteran (is_vet / is_veteran)** | A flag identifying players with 7 or more years of NBA experience (True if experience ≥ 7). |
| **Wins (W)** | The number of games a team wins in a season. |

Source: https://www.basketball-reference.com/about/glossary.html
